Allergens

Introduce these one at a time, in the morning, a few days apart. Once one is in, keep offering it a couple of times a week.

His roadmap

Wait until after his first birthday

✕Honey, raw or baked in. It can carry botulism spores.
✕Cow’s milk as a drink. It’s fine in cooking, yogurt and cheese.
✕Juice, added sugar and salt. Water from a cup with meals is fine from 6 months.
✕High-mercury fish like shark, swordfish, king mackerel and bigeye tuna.

Choking hazards to change the shape of

!Whole nuts and spoonfuls of nut butter. Thin nut butter into purée or spread it thinly.
!Whole grapes, cherry tomatoes and blueberries. Quarter or squash them.
!Raw carrot, apple chunks, popcorn and hot dog rounds. Cook until soft, grate, or cut into long strips.

Based on general guidance from the AAP (HealthyChildren.org), CDC and NIAID peanut guidelines. Every baby moves at their own pace, so check with your pediatrician.
